First time out since February. The KX2 had been sitting in the bag since a winter activation that got cut short by rain, so I was overdue.

Proud Lake is a 4,700-acre recreation area about 35 miles northwest of Detroit — easy drive, plenty of parking near the trailheads, and just enough tree coverage to hang a dipole without much effort. I've activated here twice before and the site I like is a small clearing near the boat launch where there's a decent run of trees roughly aligned east-west.

## Setup

I was parked and operating by 10:45 local. Setup took about 20 minutes — longer than I wanted, mostly because I'd forgotten I'd changed the linked dipole clip positions last fall and hadn't marked the 40m links clearly. Lesson noted: label everything.

- **Antenna:** Linked dipole slung between two oaks, about 22 feet up at the apex. Fed with about 20 feet of RG-316.
- **Radio:** KX2 on the picnic table, internal ATU, battery at full charge.
- **Logging:** Paper log on the table, transferred to ADIF after.

Spotted myself on POTA Spots just before 11:00 on 7.040.

## 40 Meters

40 was in good shape from the start. I had a pileup of three or four callers within the first few minutes after spotting — nothing crazy but enough to keep me busy. The key feel on the KX2's internal paddle took about ten minutes to get comfortable with again; my timing was sloppy early and I made a few corrections mid-QSO that I wasn't thrilled about.

By 11:30 I had 9 QSOs in the log, all 40m. Signals were generally 559–579, with a couple of loud ones from Ohio and Pennsylvania. One contact from Tennessee was weak but solid — maybe 449 on my end, and he came back to confirm he copied me 569. That's one of the things I love about CW: a contact that would be lost in the noise on phone works fine with Morse.

**S2S #1:** Around 11:45 I heard W8XYZ/P calling CQ POTA on 7.032. He was activating Pinckney Recreation Area (K-4567) about 15 miles away. Worked him easily — 579 both ways, which makes sense given the short skip.

## 20 Meters

Switched to 20 just before noon. Rough at first — noise floor was high and I could hear stations but they couldn't hear me, or vice versa. Spotted on 14.040 and waited. Got two contacts in about 20 minutes, which wasn't worth staying on.

Went back to 40 briefly, then came back to 20 around 13:45 local (1845Z). Better. The band had opened up — I worked five more stations in about 25 minutes including a station in Florida and one I couldn't fully copy but got enough for the log.

**S2S #2:** N4ABC/P on 14.042 toward the end of the 20m run. Couldn't catch his park number in the QSB but confirmed it later on POTA Spots — he was at a park in North Carolina.

## Wrap-up

Packed up at 12:53 local. 14 QSOs total, 2 S2S, qualified with room to spare. The KX2 battery was at about 70% remaining — I'd taken it off charge the night before and didn't top it off, which I probably should have.

Total time on air: 68 minutes.

## Notes for Next Time

- **Label the dipole links.** Seriously.
- Bring a ground stake. I've been going without one and the antenna base wobbles more than I'd like.
- The 3D-printed feed point holder I made in March worked well — no issues with the SO-239 connection.
- Consider a dedicated log sheet with POTA-specific fields pre-printed rather than a blank pad.

Overall: good activation, shook off the winter rust. Ready to get back into a regular cadence.
