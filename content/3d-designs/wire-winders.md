## Wire Winders

### Description
A set of cable organizers designed to keep antenna leads, power cables, and other wiring neat and tangle-free during portable operation. Compact enough to fit in a go-kit.

### Specifications
- Print time: ~1-2 hours (per winder)
- Filament: ~15-20 grams (per winder)
- Material: PLA or PETG
- Multiple size options available

### Features
- Compact storage
- Reusable design
- Works with various cable types
- Stackable
