## Leg Strap Mount For K8CES Zippy Paddle

### Description
This is a leg strap mount for a K8CES Zippy CW paddle, using standard size elastic strap and steel phone mount plates.

### Specifications
- Print time: >0.5 hours
- Filament: ~10-12 grams
- Material: PLA

### Features
-25mm Leg strap compatible
-Magnetic pad for mounting your Zippy
Lightweight portable design

### Files & Downloads
- STL file (https://www.thingiverse.com/thing:7375979)

### Assembly Notes
It accepts 25mm wide straps, like this one: https://www.amazon.com/gp/product/B09QSY9B7J/
I've also found nice elastic velcro straps at Daiso, example here: https://daisous.com/products/ec-4972822411431

The central area is sized for a standard 40mm adhesive backed steel magnet mount plate, like these: https://www.amazon.com/gp/product/B09B6YVMM2/?th=1

### Notes
To assemble, I printed this in PLA with 80% infill. Then feed the elastic strap through the slots, peel the adhesive backing off of the steel plate, and adhere it inside the circle.
