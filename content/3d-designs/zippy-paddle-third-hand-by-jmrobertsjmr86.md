## Zippy Paddle Third Hand

### Description
Useful little tool to hold your Zippy paddles in place during assembly. 

### Specifications
- Print time: ~15 minutes
- Filament: ~5 grams
- Material: PLA

### Files & Downloads
- STL file (https://www.thingiverse.com/thing:7347039)

### Notes
Fighting the magnets opposing power during assembly can be challenging. This cap fits over the ends of the Zippy paddles to help with the assembly and alignment of the cover and the base of the paddle.
