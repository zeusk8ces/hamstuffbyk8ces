## Knee Board

### Description
A versatile knee board designed for portable field operation. Perfect for using your QRP radio when no benches or tables are available. 2-inch steel disk embedded in the print offers mounting for various compatible keys.

### Specifications
- Print time: ~5.5 hours
- Filament: ~225 grams
- Material: PETG

### Features
- Integrated silicone mat for slip resistance
- Bungee cord holes for unlimited tie down options
- 2 inch steel disk for magentic key mounting
- Portable design
- Adjustable straps

- STL file available on request

### Assembly Notes
Once printed, there is a spot for the 2inch disk to be placed. It is a tight fit for obvious reasons. Press the disk into place, persuasion maybe necessary. 

There is also a recessed surface to the board. This is for the self adhesive silicone pad to sit in. Line a corner of the self adhesive strip to align with the corner of the recessed area of the board. Trim the excess with an exacto knife with caution. 

Use 1/8" bungee cord to loop through the tie down holes.

Cut a strap and attach a buckle for easy attachment to the leg and your off to the races. Now just choose your preference in portable chair and enjoy a little laid back activation!
