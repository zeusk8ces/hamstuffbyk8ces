## Zippy Case

### Description
A protective case for the Zippy paddles. Compact enough to fit in a go-kit.

### Specifications
- Print time: ~2.5 hours
- Filament: ~90 grams
- Material: PETG

### Features
- Compact storage
- Allen key storage for key adjustments
- Protective enclosure for when paddle is not in use and in a pack
- Case halves secure magnetically

### Assembly Notes
Add a small amount of super glue to the magnet sockets. Press on 6mmx2mm magnets into one case. Then place another magnet onto of the ones just placed into the case. Taking the second case half, close both case halves together to ensure proper magnet orientation. 

### Notes
This simple design protects your Zippy paddle while you're hiking to your next POTA, SOTA, or taking your gear out for Field Day while it's packed away!
